# R3: Rig, Move screen and content (report)

Branch `r3-rig-move`. Numbers come from `tools/pipeline/v5/test_rig.py` on the built data (plans/raw/test_rig-after-axial-zero.log, plans/raw/rig-metrics.md), plans/raw/measure.md and the manifest.

## AC1 is NOT met
The tear and flip limits (per muscle part: tear <= 2% of edges stretched > 2x rest, flip <= 1% of triangles flipped; PLAN 2b.7 and 2b.8) fail for these poses, in both sexes unless stated:
- **abd170, overhead, flex90: teres major tears** (23 to 30% at abd170 and overhead, 2.5% at flex90 female) and shoulder flips (serratus anterior, coracobrachialis, teres minor, teres major, 2 to 7%). There is no scapula bone to rotate the shoulder blade, so teres major is stretched.
- **hip90, squat: pectineus flips** (11 to 13% male, 1.8 to 7% female). Squat female also tears superior gemellus (3.3%).
- **elbow140, knee120: compression flips** (pronator teres 1.6 to 4.4%, lateral head of gastrocnemius 1.4 to 3.6%).
- **Met: elbow90, abd90, trunk45** (both sexes).

Bones stay rigid and the articulation gap (adjacent bone ends) changes by at most 0.45 cm in every run (limit 1 cm), so that check passes. The rigid fallback (R11) is not better overall, so smooth stays the default. Next steps below.

## 5. Decisions
From PLAN 2b and the changelog:
- **Euler order** = the prototype's: intrinsic Z (abd, side, dev, inv, roll), then X (flex, pitch), then Y (rot, twist, turn, pron, yaw). Replaces "flex, abd, rot".
- **Sign negation**: our convention is rot + = internal and ankle flex + = dorsiflexion; the prototype uses the opposite, so `fromPrototype` negates `rot` and `ankle`.
- **Anatomical vs fitted zero**: upperarm, lowerarm, hand, thigh and calf use anatomical zero (arms down, palms forward, legs vertical). Pelvis, foot and ball use their fitted standing orientation (fix round 1). After a lung showed through a chest gap at elbow 90, clavicle, spine_01 to 03, neck_01 and head also use the fitted orientation (axial-zero change).
- **Frames and root**: every joint rotates in its parent segment's frame; `root.x/y/z` is a delta from the rest pelvis; pins `none | ground | flat | toes | hands | toeL | toeR`, applied by `skeleton.ground`.
- **Joint pivots** snapped to the anatomy joint centre by sphere fits on bone ends, moved 2 to 5.6 cm; articulation check added.
- **Weights**: automatic per-vertex chain blend (4 cm smoothstep); muscles spanning several segments get a full-length gradient along their own axis (fix round 1); thorax-to-arm muscles blend sideways. Eight unmatched bones go to the nearest bone.
- **Scapula**: no bone; the clavicle rotates by (elevation - 30) / 3 above 30 degrees and the shoulder is re-solved so the arm keeps its commanded direction.
- **Fallback**: the two fix rounds failed, so R11 was built and measured (section 2); smooth stays the default.
- **Ankle adjustments**: prototype ankle dorsiflexion beyond the 20 degree AAOS limit was changed (plank and press push-up to 0, back squat and bucket squat to 20), each marked `"adjusted"` in the data. Limits unchanged.
- **Toe pins**: pins `toeL` / `toeR` for lunge and step-up; a missing pin means `toes`, as in the prototype.
- **Pull-up fix** (review): pin `hands` with `pinAt` put the lowest hand or foot at the pin height, so the pull-up hung about 2 m above the bar. Now the left wrist goes to `pinAt` (the prototype's hang) in `skeleton.ts` and `rig.py`, and the player scales `pinAt` by body size like the props, so the hands hold the bar for both sexes (plans/raw/w1-pullup.png). The FK cross-check fixture now includes the hands, toes and toeL pins.
- **Limits source**: AAOS 1965 averages only (Soucie 2011 could not be fetched, removed). Limits were not checked against a scan.
- **Player**: writes role colours into `parts[].colour` and restores them. Body part labels are off while the Body page is behind another route (fixed in T8). Workouts "Coming" lists the Task builder (R5).

## 6. Known gaps and next steps
- AC1 not met (top of this report). Without a scapula bone, teres major tears at arm elevation; adding a scapula bone (or per-muscle scapula weights) is the fix.
- Linear blend skinning collapses at compressed folds (pectineus, pronator teres, gastrocnemius). Dual-quaternion skinning or a corrective blend would address it.
- Camera framing does not follow the pose: hands leave the frame at abd170 and overhead; the squat bar is partly out of frame.
- Muscle lists in the player are provisional until the effort engine (R4).
- Fingers are not posed. Skin was not checked in the pose shots (outer depth hides it).
- Phone frame rate, the Move screen by hand (M2) and playing every variant by eye (M3) remain manual checks.
