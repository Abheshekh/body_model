## What changed
- Explore: Below button and its banner removed. `Viewer.setView` takes `front | side | back`. The pelvic camera opens only from a Programs item whose targets are all pelvic-floor parts.
- Programs "Show": shared pure `highlightPlan` (src/body/model.ts), viewer `setViewInset` and `frameIds`, sheet peek (40vh on phones) with a handle button, superficial muscles at opacity 0.35 when a deep target is selected, framing from behind for posterior targets.

- `tools/sample/` checked in, `docs/REBUILD-PLAN.md` removed, `.gitignore` gains `.DS_Store` and `*.tgz`.
